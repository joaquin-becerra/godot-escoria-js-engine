Godot Escoria JS Engine

Point & click adventures made with Godot 4 + Escoria, played in the browser by a small JavaScript engine instead of Godot's WebAssembly runtime. Each demo below is the game's own project, exported to JSON and assets. Source: godot-escoria-js-engine.

The engine is MIT licensed (see LICENSE and third-party notices). Each game keeps its own license and credits, shown on its card.

Mouse to play; Esc opens the menu. Works best in a desktop browser.
